# Fluent Forever companion research and first-slice adaptation

Reviewed official companion resources, not the whole book:

- [Gabriel Wyner, Simple Word Flashcards, 2014-05-02](https://blog.fluent-forever.com/simple-word-flashcards/):
  picture/personal associations, recordings, separate comprehension (word to
  meaning/pronunciation), production (picture to word), optional spelling cards.
- [Official translation policy](https://help.fluent-forever.com/hc/en-us/articles/360004463811-How-do-you-get-the-gist-of-something-without-translation-or-a-word-of-English-in-a-flashcard):
  translation can help initial learning; routine review cards avoid translations.
- [Spaced repetition and personal connections](https://blog.fluent-forever.com/boot-up-your-brain-with-spaced-repetition/):
  correct answers increase spacing, mistakes shorten it; personally meaningful
  images matter. Do not treat marketing retention claims as established evidence.
- [New Word Flashcards](https://blog.fluent-forever.com/new-word-flashcards/):
  context and separate prompts help less concrete vocabulary and word senses.

The user superseded the original English-on-flip request with translation-free
review. First slice: recognition, kana-only front, picture plus kana on back.
Optional front hint is recorded as assisted; a hinted remembered grade receives a
10-minute retry and does not advance its unaided-recall stage. Only a deliberate
post-flip grade changes the schedule. This simple algorithm is our design, not a
claim about Wyner's proprietary SRS. Grades measure this image-associated recognition
task, not speaking ability or production mastery. Later production cards need their
own kind-qualified IDs and independent schedules.

Auto-curation is the user's preference; use vetted licensed cached assets and
allow a build-time artwork-map override. Known abstract senses need suggestive
associations rather than misleading literal images. No proprietary deck/art is
copied. Missing vetted images are clearly labeled and cannot be graded as valid
meaning-recall cards. English source meanings stay in the journal and may inform
private curation, but are not embedded/drawn as answers. Attribution on the back
contains creators/license only, not image titles that translate the target.
